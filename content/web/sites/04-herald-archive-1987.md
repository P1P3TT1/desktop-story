## Meridian Ends Children's Sleep Study After "Incident"
meta: June 19, 1987 | By Staff Reporter

Meridian Research Center has abruptly ended a pediatric sleep study after what officials describe only as "an incident" during an overnight session last week.

The study, led by Dr. Helena Reeves, enrolled twelve children between the ages of four and seven. Parents were paid for their participation. A Meridian spokesperson said all participating children "have returned home safely," but declined to explain why the study's discharge records list thirteen.

Asked about the discrepancy, Dr. Reeves said only: "Every child who entered the program left it."

Families contacted by the Herald declined to comment.
