## Local Research Facility Under Investigation
meta: March 29, 2024 | By Jennifer Walsh

Federal authorities have launched an investigation into the Meridian Research Center following reports of unauthorized human experimentation. Multiple sources claim the facility has been conducting classified projects since the 1980s.

"We received credible reports of missing persons and unethical medical procedures," said FBI spokesperson Maria Rodriguez. The facility's director, Dr. Helena Reeves, was unavailable for comment.

Local residents report strange occurrences near the facility, including unexplained lights and individuals behaving erratically after visiting the center.

## Woman Reports Identity Theft of Unprecedented Nature
meta: March 28, 2024 | By David Chen

Police are investigating a bizarre case where a local woman claims someone is "living her life" while she sleeps. The unidentified woman provided evidence of activities she doesn't remember, including appointments, purchases, and even job applications submitted in her name.

"This goes beyond typical identity theft," said Detective Laura Morrison. "The perpetrator seems to have intimate knowledge of the victim's life, habits, and even handwriting."

The case has drawn attention from federal investigators who believe it may be connected to other similar reports nationwide.
