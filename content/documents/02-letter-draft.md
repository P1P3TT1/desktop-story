Dear Sarah,

If you're reading this, then something has happened to me. Or maybe I should say "the other me" because I'm not entirely sure which version of myself is writing this.

The experiments weren't supposed to go this far. We thought we could control it, contain it to the laboratory. Dr. Reeves assured us the quantum consciousness transfer was temporary - just a few minutes of displaced awareness.

We were wrong.

The boundaries between identities are breaking down. What started as brief glimpses into alternate versions of ourselves has become something far more dangerous. We're not just observing other timelines - we're merging with them.

I've been tracking the pattern:
- Night 1: Dreams of another life
- Night 7: Physical evidence of the "other" self
- Night 14: Lost time begins
- Night 21: Complete displacement episodes
- Night 28: [I pray I don't reach this stage]

The mirror in your bedroom - DESTROY IT. Don't look into it after midnight. That's when the barrier is thinnest. The reflection isn't always yours.

Trust no one from the project. Dr. Martinez is not who you think he is. Neither is your mother - check the photographs. Look at her eyes in the old pictures versus the recent ones.

The woman who shares your apartment is you, but from a timeline where the experiments succeeded. She's trying to take your place permanently.

There's a failsafe hidden in the lab - Room B-7, behind the mirror. But be careful. Others are watching.

If you can't find this letter, if someone else is reading it, then it may already be too late for both of us.

[UNSIGNED - TRUST NO ONE]
