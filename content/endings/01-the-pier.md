You leave the apartment at 1:12 AM. You don't look at the mirror on your way out. You don't need to. You can feel it watching.

The old pier is empty. Fog rolls in off the water, thick enough to swallow the streetlights one by one.

Then you see it. A red scarf, tied around the railing at the far end. No one is wearing it.

"You came."

The voice is behind you. You know it before you turn around, the way you know your own handwriting.

She has your face. Older, thinner, with the same small scar above her left eyebrow. Her coat is soaked through, as if she has been standing in the rain for years.

"I'm the one who left," she says. "Phase 1. They told Mom I didn't make it. They told me the same thing about you."

She presses something cold into your hand. A keycard: MERIDIAN RESEARCH - LAB B-7.

"The failsafe only works if both of us walk in together. The one in your mirror knows that. That's why she wanted you to stay home."

Across the city, the lights in your apartment switch on.

Someone sits down at your computer. Someone is opening your files, one by one. Your emails. Your diary. Your letter.

You were never the only one reading.
